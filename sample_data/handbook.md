# DocuMind Handbook

This handbook is the internal guide for the DocuMind knowledge base.

## Support hours

The support desk is open on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Escalations outside those hours go to the on-call rotation.

## Retention

Chat logs are kept for 90 days. Signed contracts are kept for 7 years. Draft documents are kept for 30 days.

## Search

Document search combines a BM25 keyword index with dense vector retrieval. The two ranked lists are merged with reciprocal rank fusion.
